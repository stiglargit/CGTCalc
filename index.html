How inflation is used: The 50% Discount method does not adjust for inflation — it taxes half the nominal gain. The Inflation Discount with 30% Minimum method CPI-indexes the cost base (cost × (1+CPI%)^years), so only the gain above inflation is taxed, with a 30% floor on that real gain.
